Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Smart Farmer Expense Manager app developed by Nelli Prasad. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Our app does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers. All the data you enter (such as farmer name, crop details, expenses, and income) is stored locally on your device's browser/app storage (Local Storage).

2. Data Usage

The information you provide is used strictly within the app to calculate your farming expenses, generate reports, and display charts. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

3. Internet Connectivity

The app operates fully offline once loaded and does not require an active internet connection to save or process your expense details.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not intentionally collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
